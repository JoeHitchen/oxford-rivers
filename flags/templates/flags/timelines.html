OURCs Flag & EA Advisory Timelines

This page displays a timeline of the historical OURCs flags and EA advisories, to the extend that they have been observed by this project, and will automatically update daily as new data comes in. Each day is represented by a single data point, taken at midnight – Changes to the OURCs flag or an EA advisory which are replaced before midnight the next day will not be reflected in this data set. This page is designed to be scraper friendly and no attributation is required.

Be aware that:

  • Light blue flag was introduced on the Isis in October 2021 and does not exist at Godstow
  • Blue flag was introduced at Godstow in May 2024
  • Most of 2023 is missing EA advisory data. The project had been mothballed and a known fault in data collection was not remedied in this time
  • Extended server maintainace lead to outages in Winter 2024
