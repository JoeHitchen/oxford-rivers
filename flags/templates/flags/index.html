Transits

As of December 2020, rowing boats are only allowed to transit a lock if there are no stream advisories on either side of that lock, and all relevant OURCs flags are green (or equivalent grey). Launches may transit locks on yellow boards (a "Caution" advisory) if a risk assessment has been approved by the RowSab, and are not restricted by the Isis (or Godstow) flag.
