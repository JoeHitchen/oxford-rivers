Transits

As of December 2020, rowing boats are only allowed to transit a lock if there are no stream advisories on either side of that lock. The Isis flag must be green if transiting either Iffley Lock or Osney Lock, and the Godstow flag must also be green if transiting Osney. Launches may transit locks on yellow boards (a "Caution" advisory) if a risk assessment has been approved by the RowSab, and are not restricted by the Isis (or Godstow) flag. These indicators consider all the lock transits needed to get to or from common destinations and the latest information we have for the reaches involved.
