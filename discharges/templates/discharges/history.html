Sewage Discharges

Every discharge we have recorded from Thames Water's storm discharge monitoring stations upstream of Oxford on the Thames and the Cherwell.

How to interpret this information

The Thames Water storm discharge monitoring stations upstream of Oxford are designed to detect when sewage is being discharged into the river. When a discharge is detected, Thames Water's system records the start time and end time of the discharge, and we collect this information from their open data portal. We check the readings from the stations roughly every fifteen minutes to confirm that they are still online and that the discharge is still active.

Active discharges are highlighted in red, while discharges that have ended in the last 48 hours are highlighted in yellow. Both are counted towards the number of recent discharges shown in the summary boxes. A station is considered to be offline if we have not been able to retrieve readings from it in over two hours. Any discharges that were active at the time it went offline will still be counted as recent discharges until it has been offline for 48 hours or connectivity is restored.
